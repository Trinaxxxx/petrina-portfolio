# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Hiring managers, art directors, and technical/pipeline leads at games, VR, and arch-viz studios evaluating Petrina Kinzel for environment-art and technical-art roles. They scan fast, want proof of real-time craft and measurable impact, and often view on desktop but may land from a phone via LinkedIn.

## Product Purpose
A single-page portfolio that positions Petrina as a Technical Environment Artist who takes CAD/Revit data into shippable, performance-budgeted real-time and VR environments, and who writes her own AI-assisted Blender tooling. Success = a reviewer leaves convinced of both the artistry and the engineering, and reaches out.

## Brand Personality
Precise, understated, technical. Three words: *exacting, cinematic, credible.* The interface should feel like a dark gallery built for 3D work — the art and the numbers do the talking, not decoration. Voice is first-person, concrete, results-led.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS/agency landing pages (cream/sand backgrounds, gradient-text headings, hero-metric template with gradient accents).
- Loud, over-animated "creative developer" sites where motion upstages the work.
- Dribbble-style identical icon-card grids.

## Design Principles
1. **Show the work, not chrome** — media and measured outcomes lead; UI recedes.
2. **Practice what you preach** — a performance artist's site must itself be fast and precise.
3. **Numbers earn trust** — surface the hardest, most specific metrics (96.5% poly reduction, 27ms→7ms).
4. **Dark gallery backdrop** — ink-black canvas so real-time renders read like lit exhibits.
5. **Restraint over flourish** — one accent system, hairline structure, deliberate motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Dark-only theme with verified contrast pairings (Ink Gallery palette). Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support is required for all scroll/reveal motion. Content must remain available without JavaScript (SEO crawlers, no-JS).
